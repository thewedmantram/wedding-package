Frequently Asked Questions

Do you travel?

Yes, we can, we love to.

What is the strength of the team that will travel to cover my wedding?

While discussing with you about your function details, we shall also go into the nitty gritty's of the crowd, your requirements in terms of the deliverables. For intimate weddings, we limit the size of the team, so that each crew member gets proper control over filming without any obstructions.

Why do you take an advance amount prior to the event?

So we're asked this very often, and we absolutely consider the client's concern regarding this. However, right from the process of documentation, to making up the team for the wedding, deciding on the equipments to the process of the deliverables, everything comes with a cost. We'd love to treat every story, whether it is a lavish destination wedding or an intimate wedding at home, with the grandeur & importance it deserves. Moreover, every aspect, some of which mentioned above, is based on the trust we place on each other.

What type of camera/equipment do you use?

We use latest technology in the market to create your memories in best possible way. Be it high end lighting equipment, super fast lenses, drones, we have it all.

How many images will you deliver?

Don't worry about the pictures, once we are with you there is no limit on the number of pictures we take we deliver everything that matters to you, we do not over promise.
